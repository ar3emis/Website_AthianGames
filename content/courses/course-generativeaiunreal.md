Dive deep into the innovative use of Generative AI to enrich the game development process within Unreal. This four-session course delves into how tools like ChatGPT and midjourney can be pivotal in conceptualizing ideas, creating rich game narratives, and even assisting in game programming with Unreal C++. Instead of direct integration, this course emphasizes the auxiliary power of AI in streamlining and enhancing the creative and coding processes.
