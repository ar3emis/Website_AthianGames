## Course Content

* Cascade vs Niagara
* Overview of Niagara Tools - Niagara terminology (modules, emitters, systems)
* Inheritance (Overrides, Dynamic Inputs, Expressions)
* HLSL functions in Niagara
* Events
* Interfaces
* Houdini export to Niagara
* Camera and Occlusion Queries
