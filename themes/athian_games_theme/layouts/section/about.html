Unreal Authorized Training Center

We are now an Unreal Authorized Training Center (UATC). As a UATC, Athian Games is committed to helping developers improve their skills and knowledge of the Unreal Engine. We offer a range of trainings and courses that are designed to help professionals and hobbyists alike learn how to effectively use the Unreal Engine to create stunning games and interactive experiences. Our training courses are led by experienced instructors who are well-versed in the Unreal Engine and have a wealth of knowledge to share with their students. They use a combination of lectures, hands-on exercises, and real-world projects to help students learn the tools and techniques they need to succeed in the industry. In addition to our regular training courses, we also offer customized training programs for companies and organizations that are looking to upskill their teams or get started with the Unreal Engine. Our team of experts will work with you to create a tailored training program that meets your specific needs and goals. Whether you are just starting out in game development or are an experienced professional looking to expand your skillset, our training courses can help you take your skills to the next level and achieve success in the industry.

Company Info

Athian Games is a game development studio that was founded in 2018 with a focus on creating high-end, story-based games using the Unreal Engine. The company is dedicated to delivering immersive and engaging gaming experiences to players around the world, and it uses the latest techniques and technologies to create games that are not only enjoyable to play, but also offer a meaningful and memorable experience.

At Athian Games, we believe that the Unreal Engine is the best platform for creating cutting-edge games that push the boundaries of what is possible. It offers a wide range of tools and features that enable us to create highly detailed and realistic environments and characters, as well as smooth and seamless gameplay mechanics. We are constantly exploring new and innovative ways to use the Unreal Engine to create the best possible gaming experiences for our players.
