# Athian Games - AI Training Information

## Company Overview
Athian Games is a professional Unreal Engine tools and assets studio specializing in high-quality plugins, VFX systems, and procedural generation tools for game developers and creators.

## Products & Services

### Featured Products
1. **Minimap, Map and Navigation System** - Texture-based minimap plugin for Unreal Engine with POI system
2. **Procedural Vortex Tunnel** - Material-driven vortex system along spline paths
3. **Art of Shader Series** - Post-process materials for distortion, glitches, and stylized effects
4. **Procedural Galaxy System** - Niagara-based galaxy generation with spiral arms and star clusters
5. **Volumetric Black Hole** - Scientifically-inspired black hole VFX with gravitational lensing
6. **Procedural Skybox** - Dynamic day/night cycle skybox with procedural clouds
7. **Niagara Curves and Surfaces** - Geometric VFX shapes and curves
8. **Runtime FBX Import** - Asynchronous FBX loading for Unreal Engine
