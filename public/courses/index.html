Sameek Kundu

Unreal Authorized Instructor

Sameek was one of the first persons to use Unreal Engine, ever since it came out in March 2014, and he has extensive knowledge on the engine, from C++ gameplay programming, to editor scripting , UI and Slate, Niagara VFX , Materials and Shaders, he is known to work on a variety of stunning projects and he has been developing contents for the marketplace since 2015.
