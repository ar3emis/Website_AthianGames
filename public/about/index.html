About

Difficult to imagine an alternate reality where I have not been a gamer, eh...

Passionate towards video games, and a computer geek from heart for as long as I can remember, I was always interested in Mathematics and Physics during my school days, and Programming, mainly ever since I stepped out of college to join the field of Software Engineering. .

But gaming, was everything for me. It was like breathing, or living. Completing difficult missions in Dragon Age, and solving challenging puzzles in Tomb Raider gave me joy and excitement comparable to how the youngest Seeker in a Quidditch team would feel, after catching his/her very first Snitch.

Sameek Kundu

Founder

Hi, I am Sameek Kundu, a Software Programmer and a Game Developer with expertise in Unreal Engine 4. I have been born and brought up in the city of joy, Kolkata. I am a solo developer who currently develops game templates, products and asset packs for the Unreal Engine 4 Marketplace. My main interest is developing high end games, specially targeting PC, Playstation and VR devices, and currently I am working on building my very first game, based on my own original story that I am writing in my free time.
